# 🌱 Code of Conduct

> Visora aims to be a respectful, constructive place for everyone building agent-facing Unity workflows.

---

## 🤝 Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Visora community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## ✅ Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and professional.
- Focus criticism on ideas and technical work, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not share credentials, private Unity projects, or other sensitive material.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and help correct mistakes.

---

## ⛔ Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without permission, or other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community is not tolerated.

---

## 📬 Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the [repository's private reporting channel](https://github.com/AmaLS367/Visora/security/advisories/new). Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or take other appropriate action on contributions and community interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.
