# 🌱 Code of Conduct

> Visora aims to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone building agent-facing Unity workflows.

---

## 🤝 Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Visora community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## ✅ Expected Behavior

- 🤝 **Respectful & Professional**: Treat community members, contributors, and maintainers with empathy and respect.
- 💡 **Constructive Dialogue**: Focus criticism on ideas, architectures, and technical implementations rather than individuals.
- 🔒 **Privacy & Safety**: Never expose API keys, internal credentials, proprietary project files, or private user data.
- 🔄 **Accountability**: Gracefully accept constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and work collaboratively towards solutions.

---

## ⛔ Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered harassment and are completely unacceptable within our community:
- Discriminatory, offensive, or derogatory language and comments.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit consent (doxxing).
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Any conduct that reasonably degrades the safety and professional integrity of the project.

---

## 📬 Enforcement & Reporting

> [!NOTE]
> Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the [Repository Security / Advisory Channel](https://github.com/AmaLS367/Visora/security/advisories/new).

Project maintainers reserve the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct.
